# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people using it.
- Keep discussion on topic and free of personal attacks.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or personal and political attacks.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is harmful.

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone is representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer, A. Sam Mohammad, at
[sammohammadwork@gmail.com](mailto:sammohammadwork@gmail.com). Reports are handled in confidence. Security vulnerabilities are a separate matter; follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for those.
