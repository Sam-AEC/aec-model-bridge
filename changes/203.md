- Two-person rule (off by default): the policy key `require_second_approver` records the requester on the plan and refuses an approval by the same person; an unknown requester or approver is refused. Names are compared ignoring case and any domain part (`alice` and `alice@corp` match). The rule is checked again at execution, so a plan with no recorded requester or approved by its requester does not run. Names come from `MCP_REVIT_REQUESTER` and `MCP_REVIT_APPROVER`, else the OS account. This is not an authentication control: a separate approver needs a separate OS account or approving machine. Fixes #203.
